About Us

Smart Tap & Automation Assistant for iPhone

Aotckkr helps you set up taps, timers and multi-step sequences, then preview them safely inside the app.

Practice inside the app

Demo sessions, the Tap Playground and Macro Studio all run in Aotckkr’s own environment. Nothing outside the app is touched.

Build sequences

Create step-by-step timelines with taps, waits, swipes and repeats. Save them as presets and reuse them with one tap.

Learn Apple’s tools

Clear guides for Switch Control and Shortcuts so you can set up real automation yourself using the features Apple provides.

What Aotckkr does

  • Demo sessions. Start, stop and track practice runs with adjustable interval, duration and start delay.
  • Presets. Save configurations (steps, interval, duration) so you can launch them quickly.
  • Sequence builder. Arrange taps, waits, swipes and repeats on a timeline and preview them.
  • Tap Playground. Place points on a simulated screen, preview the sequence, send it to the builder or save it as a preset.
  • Macro Studio. Record taps on a pad, edit delays, replay and save.
  • Gesture & scroll demos. Live demonstrations of taps, long-presses, swipes and auto-scroll previews.
  • Timer. Countdown with optional start delay and multiple rounds.
  • Statistics. Session history, action counts and simple activity charts.

What we believe

  • Honest about limits. iOS does not allow third-party apps to send taps to other apps. Aotckkr is clear about this and never claims otherwise.
  • Private by design. No account. No analytics. No ads. Your presets and history stay on your device.
  • Helpful guides. When you want real automation, we point you to Apple’s Switch Control and Shortcuts with step-by-step instructions.
  • Fair pricing. Core practice tools are free. Premium unlocks unlimited presets, advanced timing, extended analytics and more.
