Privacy Policy

Effective date: 20 November 2026 · Applies to Aotckkr for iOS

Aotckkr is a smart tap and automation assistant for iPhone. Sessions run only inside the app’s own demo environment. The app never claims to tap, swipe or record in other iOS apps.

The short version.

  • No account, no sign-in, no sign-up.
  • We do not collect, store, sell or share your personal data.
  • No analytics, no advertising, no tracking, no crash-reporting service.
  • Your presets, sequences, recordings and session history stay on your device.
  • Purchases are handled by Apple. We never see your payment details.

1. Who we are

Aotckkr is developed and published by Muhammad Hamza Ameer (“we”, “us”, “our”). You can reach us at mhamzaameer9@gmail.com for any question about this policy.

2. Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information through the app. The app has no user accounts and does not send your presets, sequences, recordings, session history or settings to us or to any other server.

If you contact us by email, we receive your email address and the content of your message. We use it only to reply to you and to improve the app, we keep it only as long as needed to handle your request, and we do not share it with anyone except where the law requires.

3. Information stored on your iPhone

To work, Aotckkr keeps the following on your device only, inside the app’s own sandbox:

  • Your presets, multi-step sequences, macro recordings and session history.
  • Your settings (theme, language, haptics, sound, notifications preference, default interval and duration).
  • Your Premium status, as confirmed by Apple.

This information never leaves your iPhone through Aotckkr. You can erase it at any time by resetting the demo data in Settings, or by deleting the app. If you back up your iPhone (for example with iCloud or a computer), the app’s data may be included in that backup, which is controlled by you and Apple, not by us.

4. Permissions

Aotckkr asks for as little as possible:

  • Notifications — optional. Only used for timer and session alerts, and only after you turn them on.
  • Haptics — uses your system setting. No special permission is required.

The app does not ask for access to your contacts, photos, camera, microphone, location, calendar, or any other personal data. It does not use Accessibility APIs to control other apps. Switch Control is set up by you in iOS Settings; Aotckkr only provides guidance.

5. Purchases and subscriptions

In-app purchases and subscriptions are handled by Apple under the Apple Media Services terms. We do not receive your name, Apple ID, card number or billing address. The app only checks with Apple, on your device, whether Premium is active so that it can unlock the features. Apple’s own privacy policy applies to that processing: apple.com/legal/privacy.

We may see aggregated sales and subscription reports from Apple. These do not identify you.

6. Internet use

Aotckkr does not need an internet connection to run demo sessions or use the playground. It connects to the internet only to talk to the App Store for purchases and, when you choose to, to open a link (such as this page) in your browser or to start an email to support in your mail app. Those apps then follow their own privacy policies.

7. Third-party software

Aotckkr does not use third-party analytics, advertising, attribution or social-media SDKs. Any open-source components run locally on your device and do not send data anywhere.

8. Sharing and selling data

Because we do not collect personal data through the app, we have nothing to sell, rent, trade or share, and we do not do any of those things. We do not use your data for advertising and we do not track you across other companies’ apps or websites.

9. Children

Aotckkr is a general-purpose utility and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children. The app contains no ads, no social features and no way to chat with other users.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the EU/UK GDPR, the UAE Federal Decree-Law on Personal Data Protection, or California law), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal data, to object to its processing, and to complain to a data-protection authority. Since we hold no personal data about you from your use of the app, there is nothing for us to access or delete. If you have emailed us and want that correspondence deleted, write to mhamzaameer9@gmail.com and we will do it. You are in full control of the data stored on your device.

11. Security

Your data stays on your device, protected by iOS and the App Sandbox. No method of storage is perfectly secure, so please keep your iPhone updated and protect it with a passcode, Face ID or Touch ID.

12. Apple’s App Privacy label

This policy matches the App Privacy information on the App Store page for Aotckkr: the app does not collect data from you.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example when the app gains a new feature. The latest version is always on this page with its effective date at the top. If a change affects how your data is handled, we will tell you in the app before it takes effect.
